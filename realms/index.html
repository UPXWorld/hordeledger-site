Every WoW Forever auction house, compared

The rest of this site follows one Horde auction house hour by hour. Once a week we photograph all of them, Alliance, Horde and neutral on every US realm, and set Forever beside the seven-year-old Classic Era economy. Snapshot of Nov 11, 2026.

What stands out

  • Two realms hold 84% of all faction auctions. Realms B and A are each about 6 times the size of the smallest.
  • Alliance and Horde pay the same on this realm. The Alliance house is level with the Horde house on core trade goods.
  • Small realms are dearer. On the quieter realms the same trade goods cost 21-32% more: fewer sellers, less undercutting.
  • The neutral auction houses are a side market. They hold 3% of all auctions and charge 64-116% more.
  • Classic Era prices are 3.3 times Forever's. Seven years of gold piling up does that; everything below compares relative prices, with that difference taken out.
  • Forever's gap between low and high tiers is wider. One Illusion Dust buys 109 Strange Dust here, against 17 in Classic Era. Low-level materials are flooded while everyone levels.
  • Herb prices look low, cloth prices look high. Against Classic Era, herb prices are 1.8 times lower in Forever than the overall price level suggests, and cloth prices are 1.6 times higher.
