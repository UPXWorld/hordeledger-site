Every WoW Forever auction house, compared

The rest of this site follows one Horde auction house hour by hour. Once a week we photograph all of them, Alliance, Horde and neutral on every US realm, and set Forever beside the seven-year-old Classic Era economy. Snapshot of Nov 25, 2026.

1,445,608auctions across WoW Forever (US)
12auction houses with listings, on 4 realms
+6%price premium on the small realms
3.5×Classic Era's price level against Forever

What stands out

  • Two realms hold 80% of all faction auctions. Realms B and A are each about 4 times the size of the smallest.
  • Alliance and Horde pay the same on this realm. The Alliance house is level with the Horde house on core trade goods.
  • Small realms are dearer. On the quieter realms the same trade goods cost 3-11% more: fewer sellers, less undercutting.
  • The neutral auction houses are a side market. They hold 6% of all auctions and charge 16-112% more.
  • Classic Era prices are 3.5 times Forever's. Seven years of gold piling up does that; everything below compares relative prices, with that difference taken out.
  • Forever's gap between low and high tiers is narrower. One Dreamfoil buys 11 Peacebloom here, against 649 in Classic Era.
  • Herb prices look low, cloth prices look high. Against Classic Era, herb prices are 1.9 times lower in Forever than the overall price level suggests, and cloth prices are 1.7 times higher.
