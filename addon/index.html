HordeLedger addon

The prices on this site, on your item tooltips in game. Hover an item to see what it sells for on the Horde auction house, how many are listed, how fast it sells and whether now is a good time to post.

What it does

  • Tooltip prices. Cheapest listing, market price, units listed, estimated sales per day, and where the price sits in its 7-day range.
  • Posting tips. When undercutting the cheapest listing would be a mistake (a few cheap listings in front of a wall, a price spike, a slow seller, or a listing under vendor price), the tooltip says so.
  • Vendor check. If a vendor sells the item, the tooltip shows the vendor price next to the auction house price.
  • Market window. The price index, the day's biggest movers, and how this house compares with the other Forever auction houses.
  • Site links. Right-click an item in the window for its page here, with charts and history.

It only reads and shows prices. It never posts, buys, cancels or automates anything, and it sends nothing anywhere.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it. You get one folder called HordeLedger.
  2. Close the game. Open your World of Warcraft folder, then the WoW Forever game folder inside it, then Interface, then AddOns.
  3. Move the HordeLedger folder in there and start the game. On the character screen, check that HordeLedger is ticked under AddOns.

Keeping prices fresh

Addons can't use the internet, so the prices are a snapshot from when you downloaded. This site takes a new snapshot every hour. To refresh:

  • double-click Update Prices.cmd inside the HordeLedger folder (Windows; it downloads one file, Data.lua, from this site), or
  • download the zip again and replace the folder.

Then type /reload in game. The addon tells you how old its prices are when you log in, and warns you after a day.

Commands

TypeWhat it does
/hlOpen the market window: price index, biggest movers, this auction house against the other Forever houses.
/hl <item>Price check in chat. A name, part of a name, or shift-click an item.
/hl web <item>The item's page on this site, in a box you can copy. Addons can't open a browser.
/hl tooltipTurn the tooltip lines on or off.

Which prices

WoW Forever · US PvP · Horde, the same auction house as the rest of this site. Other realms and the Alliance auction house have different prices, so use it as a guide there, not a quote. How the numbers work.
