Journeyman Crates Up Sevenfold, Artisan Crates Down 99%

The week's biggest moves on both sides of the board come from one item family. The typical Journeyman-tier Waylaid Crate went from 2s 55c to 17s 42c between November 9 and this morning, close to seven times the price. Over the same week the typical Artisan-tier crate went from 2g 44s to 2s 02c, a fall of 99%. The Sealed Writs show the same split by tier.

Four tiers, two directions

There are about 30 Waylaid Crates on the Horde auction house, seven or eight per tier (ore, herbs, ingots, textiles, fabrics, parts, curiosities). Taking the middle crate of each tier, using each day's typical market price, gives this:

It is not one outlier dragging a tier. All seven Journeyman crates rose, from +109% (Curiosities) to well over tenfold (Ingots, though most of that jump came this morning, so treat it as provisional). All seven Apprentice crates rose. All eight Artisan crates fell, by 81% to 99%.

Both charts start with launch-week asking prices, which were high and thin. The part to read is from November 9 on: Journeyman Herbs climbing every single day, Artisan Textiles stepping down from 90s to 2s 15c.

Supply explains the top, demand the bottom

The Artisan fall is a supply story. On November 9 there were 117 Artisan crates listed in total, with single sellers setting prices of several gold. Today there are about 1,400. Those early prices were never a real market; this week is the first time the tier has had one.

The low tiers are different. Prices doubled or more while the number listed went down slightly. That points to buyers clearing the shelf rather than sellers holding back. Each Journeyman crate shows an estimated 440 to 1,060 units a day leaving the house; that figure includes cancelled auctions, so it is a ceiling.

One more pattern worth knowing: in every tier the Fabrics crate is the expensive one. Apprentice Fabrics sits at 23s 40c, Journeyman at 48s 32c, Expert at 58s 62c and Artisan at 94s 99c, the only Artisan crate that has held near a gold.

How sure is this

Thirteen days of history, and these are asking prices, not sales. Most crates have 100 to 500 listed, enough to trust the direction, but a few are thin this morning: Apprentice Ore has 8 listed and Journeyman Curiosities 14, so their latest quotes are one seller's ask. Today's figures cover scans up to 09:00 only. Crates and writs are Forever items with no Classic equivalent to lean on, and we have not verified what they are handed in for or what they pay, so this post reads the prices only. The cause of the low-tier demand is our inference from price and stock, not something the data states.

What to do

  • Holding Apprentice or Journeyman crates or writs: these are worth several times last week's price. Check the item page before vendoring or giving them away.
  • Buying Artisan crates: most now cost 2s to 6s against gold-level asks a week ago. Stock is still growing, so there is no rush.
  • Selling Artisan crates: the gold-plus prices are gone except for Fabrics. Price to the current lowest listing or expect to wait.
  • Before buying a thin crate: look at the listed count. Under 10 listed, the price is whatever one seller typed.
