Firebloom and Sungrass Tripled at Dawn. Such Spikes Rarely Last a Day

At the 06:43 scan, Firebloom was 39s 93c, up from about 12s an hour earlier, and three other materials jumped with it. That looks like a week high, and it is. But we checked 895 jumps like this since November 14, and 71% were back near the old price within 12 hours. If you hold these, the window to sell is short. If you need them, waiting is usually cheaper.

"Before" is the median of the eight scans ahead of the jump, roughly the previous eight hours.

In Sungrass and Gromsblood the cheap stock disappeared between two scans and only a few dozen units were left at higher asks. Firebloom is different: 1,727 were listed with nothing under 39s. We only see listings, so we can't tell bought from cancelled, and we can't tell whether anything sells at the new price. These are asks from one scan.

How long a spike lasts

We looked at 427 fast-selling trade goods and consumables (an estimated 200 or more sold a day, an upper bound) and counted every time the price at least doubled against its previous eight scans. That happened 130 to 200 times a day. The typical jump was 3.0x. For the 895 that are at least a day old:

So more than half of the extra price is gone in three hours, and about one spike in nine sticks for longer than a day. Seven days of data is a short record, and it covers one week of a young realm.

Why they fade: sellers show up

A high price is an advert to everyone sitting on a pile of herbs. In spikes that have ended, listings grew to a median of 4.8 times the count at the jump. Arthas' Tears yesterday is the clean example: 18s 90c with 183 listed, then 69s 98c with 94 listed at 11:00. Nine hours later 2,285 were listed, and by 21:00 the price was 20s 83c.

These four have done this before. Sungrass hit 30s 39c on November 14 and 35s 44c on November 16, and was back near 12s in 10 and 13 hours. Living Essence went to 2g 94s on November 15 and 2g 17s on November 17, and took 17 and 9 hours to return. Firebloom's last jump, to 30s 95c on November 19, took about a day. The daily chart barely shows any of them, which is the point.

What to do

  • Holding Firebloom, Sungrass, Gromsblood or Living Essence: list it now if the price is still up when you log in. A 20-stack of Firebloom is 7g 80s at this morning's ask against 2g 40s yesterday, before the 5% cut. Price just under the cheapest listing; in three hours the typical spike has lost more than half its gain.
  • Don't expect the top price. With 1,727 Firebloom listed and at most about 2,000 a day leaving the shelf, later sellers will undercut. Living Essence at 4g 78s is 17 times its usual price and rests on 109 units.
  • Buying for alchemy or leveling: wait half a day and look again. Seven times in ten the old price is back by then.
  • Thinking of buying to relist higher: the numbers are against holding. Supply answers within hours, and you pay a deposit on every unsold stack.
  • Farmers: keep a few stacks of fast-selling herbs in reserve rather than dumping them all at once. On this record, some item you carry doubles on most days, and you need to be the one listing in the first hours.
