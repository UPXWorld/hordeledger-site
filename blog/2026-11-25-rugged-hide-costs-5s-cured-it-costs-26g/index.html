Rugged Hide Costs 5s. Cured, It Costs 26g. The Gap Is Salt

The cheapest Rugged Hide on the Horde auction house this morning was 5s 00c, which is exactly what a vendor pays for one. A Cured Rugged Hide was 26g 45s 98c, about 529 times as much. The hide is the cheap part of that recipe, and it has been sitting on the floor for two weeks.

Three hides at the vendor floor

A price cannot stay below what a vendor pays for long, because anyone can buy the listing and sell it to a vendor. Three hides have spent most of the past week pressed against that line.

Rugged Hide's typical daily price has stayed between 5s 03c and 5s 92c since November 16. Roughly 3,000 are posted a day and at most 1,700 leave, so the shelf refills faster than it empties. That "leaving" figure counts units that vanish between scans, which includes cancelled listings, so treat it as an upper bound.

The ladder is upside down

The odd part is what the lower-level hides cost. Medium Hide was 6s 13c and Heavy Hide 12s 97c at the same scan. Medium Hide's typical price went from 3s 08c on November 17 to 8s 95c on November 24. A mid-level hide now costs more than the top-tier one.

Leather is not following the hides down either. Rugged Leather went from a typical 8s 64c on November 10 to 19s 73c on November 24, with over 7,000 listed. Leatherworkers are clearly buying at the top tier. They just are not buying raw hides.

Why: the salt, not the hide

In Classic, curing a Rugged Hide takes one Refined Deeprock Salt, and that salt comes from a Salt Shaker on a three-day cooldown. However many hides skinners bring in, only so many can be cured. The prices here fit that picture.

One thing does not add up. The salt costs almost twice as much as the cured hide it is supposed to go into, and it has cost more than the cured hide on every day in our record. Under the Classic recipe nobody would cure at those prices. Either Forever's recipe gives more per salt, or the salt has another use here. We have not verified which, so check the recipe in game before acting on it.

Both are also coming down. Cured Rugged Hide's typical price was 35g 83s on November 22 and 26g 45s today; the salt went from 66g 23s to 48g 65s over the same days.

One tier down the same shape shows in miniature. Thick Hide at 5s 10c plus Deeprock Salt at 7s 50c comes to 12s 60c, against 11s 00c for a Cured Thick Hide. Deeprock Salt was near 3s for most of the past week and jumped overnight, so that margin flips with the salt price.

How sure is this

The hide prices are solid: 22 days of hourly scans and over a thousand listed of each. The cured hide and refined salt are smaller markets (166 and 111 listed) with wide daily swings. All prices are asks, not completed sales, and the recipes are Classic's.

What to do

  • Skinners: the auction house takes a 5% cut. A Rugged or Thick Hide has to sell above 5s 27c to beat the vendor's 5s 00c; a Pristine Hide above 2s 64c. At this morning's prices, vendoring all three paid more than undercutting.
  • Leatherworkers with a Salt Shaker: the hide is the cheapest it can get, so the cooldown is where the value sits. Compare selling the salt with selling the cured hide before each use.
  • Buyers of cured hides: the price has fallen for three days running. Buy what the next craft needs, not a pile.
  • Anyone holding hides for a rebound: the downside is zero at 5s 00c, but supply has outrun demand every day for two weeks. Low risk is not the same as likely gain.
