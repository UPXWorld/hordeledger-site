Blue Dragonscale Fell 98% in Nine Days. Black Still Costs 16 Times More

Blue Dragonscale first showed up on the Horde auction house on November 10 at 4g 8s. This morning it asks 7s 85c with 215 listed: a 98% fall, with no bounce on the way down. A vendor pays 5s for it, so the floor is close. The odd part is the scale next to it: Black Dragonscale is still 1g 30s and sells faster than any of the cheap ones.

A price that roughly halved every day

Most launch-month price drops come in steps. This one was a slide. Each day's median price was lower than the last, and each day there was more on the shelf.

From November 12 to today the price fell by a factor of about 50 in seven days. That works out to one halving roughly every 30 hours. The first three days rest on fewer than 20 units listed, so the 4g starting price was a handful of asks, not a deep market. From November 13 on, with 50 to 240 listed and 22 or more scans a day, the trend is solid.

The cause is visible in the flow numbers. Our estimate is that at most about 144 Blue Dragonscales leave the auction house per day, while about 318 arrive. When twice as much comes in as goes out, sellers undercut each other until something stops them.

What stops them: the vendor

That something is the vendor price. Two of the five dragonscales have already reached it, and Blue and Red are next in line.

Green and Worn have sat at 5s since about November 14. At that price the auction house is the worse deal: after the 5% cut, a 5s sale pays 4s 75c, and the vendor pays the full 5s with no wait. Blue at 7s 84c still nets about 7s 45c, which is 2s 45c better than the vendor. Red went from 8g on November 13 (15 listed) to 19s today, and nets about 3s over its vendor price.

Black is the exception

Black Dragonscale also fell, from about 4g on November 9 (4 listed) to 1g 30s now. But it has stayed between 1g 30s and 2g 6s for five days while stock grew from about 240 to about 400. It turns over faster than the others too: up to about 515 a day against 381 listed, where Green sells at most 671 a day against more than 1,000.

In Classic, Black Dragonscale goes into fire-resistance leather gear that raiders want, which would explain steady buying ahead of the December 9 raid launch. We haven't verified those recipes in Forever, so treat that as a likely reason, not a measured one. What the scans do show is demand: people are paying for Black, and almost nobody is paying up for the rest.

What to do

  • Skinners with Green or Worn: sell to a vendor. The auction house pays less after the cut.
  • Skinners with Blue or Red: the auction house still beats the vendor by 2s to 3s a scale, but the gap has shrunk every day. Check the lowest listing before you post, and vendor anything that would net under 5s (Blue) or 15s (Red).
  • Skinners choosing a target: one Black Dragonscale is worth about 16 Blue right now, and it's the one that sells.
  • Leatherworkers: Blue, Green and Worn are as cheap as they can get. If a pattern you own uses them, materials are no longer the cost that matters.
