Smelting Pays More the Higher the Skill Gate

Ten days in, most crafts on the Horde auction house sell for about what their materials cost, or less. Smelting is the exception, and only at the top. Buying Thorium Ore and selling Thorium Bar has cleared about 7s 05c per bar after the auction cut at today's median prices, a 39% return on the ore. Copper and tin clear nothing.

The ladder

Each row uses today's median market price across eight scans, one ore per bar as in Classic, and takes the 5% auction cut off the bar. Skill levels are Classic's.

The margin climbs with every step. That fits a young realm: nearly every miner can smelt copper, so the bar is worth the ore and no more. Far fewer have reached 250, so whoever can smelt thorium is paid for it.

How solid is the thorium margin?

Fairly solid, but shrinking. Thorium has only five days of history here. The daily net was about 24s 83c on November 10, 9s 82c on the 11th, 5s 06c on the 12th and 7s 05c today. In all eight of today's scans the cheapest bar, less the cut, beat the cheapest ore, by between 2s 58c and 7s 94c. At 07:00 that gap was 5s 17c (ore 16s 66c, bar 22s 98c).

Both sides are deep markets: 4,835 ore and 2,476 bars listed at 07:00, with up to about 950 bars a day leaving the shelf. That sold figure includes cancelled auctions, so treat it as a ceiling. The trend over four days is that more players are reaching 250 and the gap is closing.

Mithril: real, but small

Mithril has paid something every day since November 8, between 4c and 78c per bar. At 07:00 the bar jumped to 10s 70c, which would be 2s 66c a bar. That is one scan, and the bars listed had fallen from 1,180 to 442 in an hour. The six scans before it all sat near 8s 65c. Plan around 58c, not 2s 66c.

Two smelts that lose money

Silver Bar at 2s 86c sells for well under Silver Ore at 4s 63c, a loss of about 1s 91c per smelt, and it has lost money every day this week. Gold Bar loses about 43c. The likely reason is that these smelts are being done for skill points and the bars sold for whatever they fetch, though the scans can't confirm motive.

The same pattern outside mining

Potions show the low-gate side. Greater Healing Potion lists at 4s 16c against about 14s 80c of herbs and a vial in Classic's recipe. Heavy Runecloth Bandage shows the high-gate side: 23s 99c against 14s 90c for two Runecloth, about 7s 89c net. It needs First Aid 260 in Classic. That market is thin, with 65 listed, four days of history and at most 80 a day sold.

What to do

  • Miners at 250: smelt thorium before selling. On today's numbers the bar is worth about 5s to 7s more than the ore after the cut.
  • Miners at 175 to 249: mithril smelting adds roughly 58c a bar. Worth it if you're at the forge anyway; not worth buying ore for.
  • Buying ore to smelt: only thorium and truesilver leave room for a bad scan. Check both prices the same hour, since the thorium gap has halved more than once this week.
  • Need silver or gold bars: buy the bars. They're cheaper than the ore.
  • Need healing potions: buy them ready-made while they sell under herb cost.
