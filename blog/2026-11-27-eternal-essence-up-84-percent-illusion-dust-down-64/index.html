Eternal Essence Up 84% in Two Weeks. Illusion Dust Fell 64%

The two materials that come out of the same disenchanted level-50+ gear have gone opposite ways on the Horde auction house. Greater Eternal Essence costs 3g 47s at the 07:00 scan, up from a daily median of 1g 89s on November 13. Illusion Dust went from 57s to about 20s over the same days. Because top-end enchants lean on essence, Enchant Chest - Greater Stats now costs 53g 35s in materials, 26% more than a week ago.

What the enchants cost today

These use Classic's recipes and the cheapest listings at 07:00. Enchants are not sold on the auction house, so this is the material bill only, not a profit figure.

Every enchant that rose has Eternal Essence or Essence of Water in it. Crusader got cheaper because it uses neither: Righteous Orb fell 22% in a week to 8g 81s, which outweighed a dearer Large Brilliant Shard (3g 88s).

Essence is the bill

Greater Stats takes 10 Greater Eternal Essence, 4 Large Brilliant Shards and 15 Illusion Dust in Classic. At today's prices the essence is 34g 76s of the 53g 35s, about 65%. The 15 dust come to 3g 07s. Dust could go to zero and the enchant would still cost over 50g.

The rise is not a shortage. Greater Eternal listings grew from about 170 on November 13 to between 430 and 750 a day this week, and the price climbed anyway. Up to about 850 a day leave the shelf (that count includes cancelled auctions, so it is a ceiling). More supply and a higher price together point to buyers arriving faster than sellers. Raids open on December 9, and players enchanting gear ahead of that is the obvious candidate, though price data cannot prove a motive.

Dust went the other way

Illusion Dust listings went from 273 to 2,757 over the same two weeks, and Dream Dust slid from 24s to 17s. In Classic, disenchanted armor mostly gives dust while weapons mostly give essence, so a flood of unwanted armor would produce exactly this split. That is a reading of the pattern, not something the scans can confirm.

Essence of Water: the sharper move

Superior Strength needs six Essence of Water in Classic. It was 4g 18s on November 21 and is 6g 66s now, a 59% rise in six days, with 331 listed. Those six essences are 39g 96s of the 41g 19s bill. It has only been this high for two days, so treat it as a spike until it holds.

One small saving

In Classic an enchanter can split one greater essence into three lesser. Three Lesser Eternal Essence cost 3g 62s at 1g 20s 50c each; one Greater costs 3g 47s 58c. If the recipe wants lessers, buying greaters and splitting saves about 14s per three, roughly 4%.

What to do

  • Getting an enchant done: price the essence yourself before you agree a fee. Greater Stats is a 53g job in materials today, not the 42g it was last week.
  • Enchanters buying Lesser Eternal: check the Greater first. It was the cheaper route at 07:00, but the gap is small and flips easily.
  • Disenchanting level-50+ greens: the essence is where the value is. Illusion Dust at 20s with 2,757 listed is a crowded shelf; list it in small lots or keep it for your own skill-ups.
  • Strength bracers: Essence of Water is two days into a spike. If the enchant can wait, watch whether 6g holds.
  • Levelling Enchanting: the low end is cheap and getting cheaper. Strange Dust is 1s 27c with over 8,000 listed.
