Five Cheap Staples Repriced Overnight

Between 2 and 7 this morning, five low-priced, fast-selling items on the Horde auction house jumped to between 3 and roughly 1,900 times yesterday's price. The pattern was the same each time: the units listed dropped sharply, then the next scan showed a new, much higher price. Small Venom Sac went from 1s 00c to 3s 83c and has held there for five scans. Incense Candle went from 4c to 76s 37c, and that one deserves more doubt.

What the scans show

None of these items had moved much all week. Small Venom Sac had a daily median between 92c and 1s 14c every day since November 5. Brilliant Smallfish sat between 12c and 18c. Then, in a single night:

In every row the shelf emptied first. Fish Bowl fell to 11 units at 02:27 while still priced at 1s 40c. An hour later there were 197 listed at 4s 88c. That is what it looks like when the cheap listings are bought or pulled and whatever comes next is posted higher. The scans can't tell us who did it or why, and they can't separate purchases from cancelled auctions.

Two that are holding, three that are unproven

Small Venom Sac and Fish Bowl are the most believable moves. Both have stayed near 4s across four or five scans, and sellers have responded: listings grew to 837 and 841, about double a normal day. Prices are already easing under that supply. Venom Sac peaked at 4s 86c at 03:43 and is 3s 83c now. Fish Bowl peaked at 4s 88c and is 3s 98c.

The other three rest on one or two scans. Elemental Water was 11s 07c at 06:00 and 63s 20c at 07:00, with 178 listed. Its sell-through estimate is up to about 590 a day, so the market is real, but one scan is not a trend. Brilliant Smallfish is the odd one: the cooked fish was selling below the raw fish yesterday (15c against 90c for Raw Brilliant Smallfish), and it now asks 3s 91c against 1s 05c raw. Incense Candle at 50s to 99s after a week at 3c to 8c is an asking price nobody has to pay. Treat it as a listing, not a value, until it survives a day.

A sixth case, Goretusk Snout, shows 1g 1s after a week near 1s. Only 13 are listed, so that's a handful of asks and not much of a market.

Why cheap items are easy to reprice

At 4c each, yesterday's median stock of 181 Incense Candles cost about 7s to clear. Even 436 Small Venom Sacs at 1s was only about 4g 36s. When the whole shelf costs a few gold, any buyer with a use for the item, or a plan to relist it, can empty it. The new price then lasts only as long as fresh supply stays away. For the venom sacs and fish bowls, supply returned within about three hours.

What to do

  • If you hold any of these: the venom sac and fish bowl prices have real listings behind them now, but they're drifting down as stock piles up. Listing sooner beats waiting.
  • If you need them: don't pay the new ask for Incense Candle, Elemental Water or Brilliant Smallfish unless you need it this hour. Check again after a few more scans.
  • Cooks: at this morning's prices, cooking Raw Brilliant Smallfish (1s 05c) into the 3s 91c cooked fish would pay, in Classic's recipe. With one scan behind it, test with a small batch first.
  • Everyone: on items under a few silver, check the item page's listed count before trusting a price jump.
