Raids Open in 15 Days. Protection Potions Just Fell Up to 86%

The first raids open December 9, and the usual expectation is that raid consumables climb as the date gets closer. So far the Horde auction house is doing the opposite. Greater Nature Protection Potion went from 4g 00s a week ago to 55s 96c this morning, a fall of 86%, and almost every other raid potion and elixir is cheaper than it was seven days ago.

The protection potions

These potions only reached the auction house between November 13 and 18, so they are still finding their level. "Week ago" is the typical market price on November 17 (the median of that day's scans); "Now" is the cheapest listing at 08:00 today.

The Arcane potion shows how fast this can go. It held near 1g for five days, then dropped every few hours from Saturday evening: 79s 79c on Saturday night, 50s on Sunday night, 25s 63c early today. The Nature potion took its latest step this morning, from 69s 96c to 55s 96c between the 07:00 and 08:00 scans.

Fire and Shadow have held up best, and they are also the two with the most stock and the steadiest turnover. Fire moves up to about 475 a day and Shadow about 330, against roughly 90 for Frost. Those figures count units that vanish between scans, which includes cancelled listings, so read them as upper bounds.

Supply explains most of it. Major Mana Potion stock roughly doubled in the week, from about 450 to 851 listed, and Major Healing Potion sees about 2,300 units posted a day against at most 1,240 leaving. The two melee elixirs are the exceptions: both rose about 30%, though each has swung that much inside a single week before, so call it a lean and not a trend.

Why no pre-raid run-up yet

The likeliest reading is that alchemists are levelling and stocking ahead of the raids faster than anyone is drinking the results. Raid potions are bought to be used on raid night, and there is no raid night until December 9. Until then the buyers are mostly dungeon groups and people stocking early, while every new alchemist adds to the shelf.

Whether demand catches up when the doors open is not something this data can answer. Which resistances matter depends on Forever's own encounters, and two of the three raids are not Classic's. In Classic, Onyxia made fire protection the first potion raiders reached for; it may be no accident that Fire is holding its price best here, but that is a guess.

How sure is this

The direction is solid: 21 days of hourly scans, and most of these markets have hundreds listed. The Arcane and Frost potions are thin at 20 and 25 listed, so their "now" price is a handful of sellers' asks and could jump on one purchase. All prices are asks, not completed sales.

What to do

  • Raiders stocking up: there has been no penalty for waiting. Each of the past seven days was cheaper than the one before for most of this list. Buy what you need for now and watch the item pages as December 9 nears.
  • Alchemists: check the potion's price against its herbs before each batch. A potion that lost 70-85% in a week may no longer cover its materials.
  • Sellers holding stock for raid week: that is a bet on demand arriving faster than supply, and supply has won every day so far. Fire and Shadow have the firmest floor; Arcane and Frost the weakest.
  • Everyone: thin potions swing hard. Look at the listed count, not just the price.
