Seven Leathers Jumped Up to 17x Before Dawn. Most Are Sliding Back

Between the 05:00 and 07:00 scans this morning, the cheap listings of seven specialty leathers vanished from the Horde auction house one after another. Heavy Scorpid Scale went from 9s 29c to 79s 94c in an hour, and Chimera Leather from 6s 99c to 92s 92c. If you have any of these in your bags, this is a selling window, but a short one: in 37 earlier jumps like this since November 14, half were over within 5 hours and every one within a day.

The pattern is the same each time. The number of units listed drops sharply in one scan (Fel Leather from 1,775 to 318, Heavy Scorpid Scale from 894 to 253), and the price that remains is whatever the higher-priced listings were asking. These are asking prices. The scans cannot tell a purchase from a cancelled auction, so they do not show who cleared the shelf or why, and they do not show that anything sold at the peak.

The early ones show how it ends

The three leathers that jumped at 05:00 already had two hours to settle. Worn Dragonscale was back at 5s 05c one scan later. Fel Leather fell from 74s 58c to 19s 58c in an hour and to 14s 89c in two, as listings climbed back from 318 to 1,843. Devilsaur Leather gave back about three quarters of its jump.

Heavy Scorpid Scale is on the same road. At its 06:00 peak there were 253 listed. An hour later there were 1,291, with the cheapest at 73s 80c. Sellers are arriving faster than the price is falling, for now.

Heavy Scorpid Scale and Fel Leather were the slow ones (typically 8 to 11 hours); Devilsaur and Frostsaber usually reversed within the hour. Thirty-seven cases over two weeks is a small sample, and it describes the past, not today.

What a sale would be worth

A stack of 20 Heavy Scorpid Scale at yesterday's typical 12s 49c is about 2g 50s. At 73s 80c it is 14g 76s, or 14g 02s after the 5% auction cut. Up to about 3,090 scales a day leave the shelf at normal prices, and that count includes cancelled auctions, so it is a ceiling. Nothing in the data says buyers pay eight times as much. What to do

  • Holding Heavy Scorpid Scale, Chimera or Frostsaber Leather: list now, just under the cheapest ask, with the shortest duration. If it has not sold by the time the price is back near normal, you have lost a small deposit.
  • Do not farm for it. By the time you have skinned a stack, the odds say the price is back. This rewards stock already in the bags.
  • Ironfeather, Fel Leather, Devilsaur: the window is mostly shut. Ironfeather has 4,292 listed and a cheapest ask of 5s; do not post at the 21s peak and wait.
  • Leatherworkers buying: wait. Every earlier jump in these seven was gone within 24 hours. Frostsaber at 50s with 50 listed is a thin shelf, not a new price.
  • Thinking of buying to resell at the peak: you would be buying at the top of a move that has reversed every time so far.
