Starter Herbs Doubled in a Week While Endgame Herbs Fell

The five herbs a new Herbalist picks first all cost about twice what they did a week ago. Peacebloom went from 1s 10c on November 16 to 2s 01c on November 22 (typical price across each day's scans), and sat at 2s 22c at 07:00 today. Over the same week Stranglevine, a level-60 herb, lost 55%. This is a steady climb over ten days, not a one-morning spike.

The bottom of the herb list is the hot end

These are deep markets: thousands of units listed at any hour, so the prices are not one seller's ask. The figures below are the middle price of all scans on each day, which filters out short spikes.

Go back to November 12 and the move is bigger: Mageroyal was 71c then, so it has more than tripled, and Silverleaf was 24c. The climb came in small steps on most days, with the biggest one on November 22.

The odd result is the ordering. Peacebloom, the first herb in the game, now costs more than Bruiseweed (99c yesterday) and Wild Steelbloom (1s 08c), which in Classic need 100 and 115 Herbalism to pick. Those two barely moved all week.

Buyers, not empty shelves

Stock did not vanish. Peacebloom had about 3,500 listed on November 16 and about 3,600 yesterday; Silverleaf slipped from 5,500 to 4,800. Prices doubled on roughly the same shelf, which points to more buying rather than a shortage.

Turnover agrees. Up to about 11,800 Silverleaf and 4,900 Peacebloom leave the house each day. That is an upper bound, since it counts cancelled auctions, but even at half that rate the whole Silverleaf shelf is replaced daily. In Classic these herbs feed the first 100 points of Alchemy (Peacebloom and Silverleaf for Minor Healing Potions, Briarthorn for the next tier), so a fresh round of people leveling the profession would fit. The data cannot prove who is buying. It also matches yesterday's finding that tin, wool and low-level leather rose this week.

More level-60 gatherers means more supply up there, and fewer people passing through the starting zones. Twenty Peacebloom (about 44s) are still worth far less than twenty Dreamfoil (about 3g 55s), but the gap closed a lot in seven days.

How sure is this

Twenty days of history, with 24 to 34 scans a day last week, so the daily figures are solid. Today's column is a single 07:00 scan in a quiet hour. Short spikes are common and do not last: Peacebloom showed 10s 33c at 08:00 yesterday with 465 listed, and was back to 2s 54c three hours later with 7,700 listed. The rise in the table is the level underneath those spikes. Nothing says it continues.

What to do

  • Low-level Herbalists: stop vendoring or skipping the starter herbs. Silverleaf at 62c is six times its 10c vendor value, and it sells fast.
  • Leveling Alchemy: the first 100 points cost about double last week's price. Picking your own is worth more than it was, and Bruiseweed and Wild Steelbloom are still cheap for the next tier.
  • Buying in a spike: wait an hour or two. Starter-herb shelves refill quickly and the price comes back.
  • Selling level-60 herbs: Stranglevine and Plaguebloom are still sliding with growing shelves. Holding has cost money every day this week.
