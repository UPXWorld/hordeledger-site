Flasks Cost 16g to Brew, and the Margin Is Shrinking by the Day

A flask now costs about 16g in materials on the Horde auction house, down from 20g to 25g a week ago, because Black Lotus has fallen to 9g 48s. Flasks still sell above that, so brewing pays: about 2g 80s on a Flask of Supreme Power after the auction cut at the 07:00 scan. Two days ago the same flask paid about 8g 24s.

What a flask costs today

These use Classic's recipes (one Black Lotus, 30 of one herb, 10 of a second material and a Crystal Vial), the cheapest listings at 07:00, and take the 5% cut off the flask.

Black Lotus is 59% of the bill. The 30 Dreamfoil in a Supreme Power come to 5g 48s; the lotus alone is 9g 48s. So the flask business is mostly a bet on one herb.

Why the lotus fell

Supply arrived. On November 9 there were two Black Lotus listed at 45g each. This morning there were 280, and the daily median price has dropped every few days since: 13g 56s on the 13th, 10g 05s on the 16th, 9g 49s today. Up to about 200 a day leave the shelf, though that count includes cancelled auctions, so treat it as a ceiling.

The price is not steady inside a day. Over the last 24 hours the cheapest lotus ranged from 8g 98s to 35g 98s, with a median of 10g 61s. The high end is what you see in the hour after the cheap ones sell out.

The margin is being competed away

Flasks first appeared here on November 14, and their prices have fallen faster than the materials since. Supreme Power is the one with enough listings to trust:

That is a 10g drop in the flask in three days while materials barely moved. At today's flask price, Supreme Power breaks even with Black Lotus at about 12g 28s. The lotus's daily median sat above that as recently as November 13, and single scans in the last 24 hours went well past it.

Titans shows the fattest margin at 6g 81s, but read it carefully. Only 15 are listed and at most about 7 a day sell, against roughly 34 a day for Supreme Power. Titans was 50g on November 15 and 28g yesterday. Six days of history on a market this thin is a snapshot.

Cheaper consumables tell the usual story

Lower down, most potions still sell for less than their herbs. Major Mana Potion costs 84s 69c to make and lists at 78s 99c, although its materials have halved from 1g 70s in a week. Elixir of the Mongoose is close to even: 70s 44c in, about 5s 54c net. Greater Fire Protection Potion nets about 13s 10c, but only 10 were listed at 07:00 and it was 5g a week ago.

What to do

  • Alchemists with a flask recipe: brewing pays today, but price the lotus and the flask in the same hour. Every 1g you overpay for lotus is 1g off a margin that is already under 3g on two of the three.
  • Buying lotus: the 24-hour median is 10g 61s. If the cheapest listing is far above that, the cheap stack has probably just sold. With 280 listed and more arriving daily, checking again later costs little.
  • Raiders buying flasks: at 19g 51s to 23g 99s, the flask is about 2g to 7g over material cost. Buying ready-made is reasonable if you have no alchemist.
  • Herbalists: Black Lotus is still worth more than 50 Dreamfoil. Its fall has slowed this week, but listings have grown from about 90 to about 280 in five days, so sell rather than hold.
  • Need mana or healing potions: buy them. They sell under herb cost.
